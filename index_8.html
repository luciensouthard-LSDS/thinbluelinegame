Our Story

The Tradition Returns

Sheriff Nick Chimienti Jr. is proud to announce the return of the Thin Blue Line Football Game, a tradition that honors the courage, sacrifice, and legacy of our fallen law enforcement officers.

After several years away, this special event is finally returning. The game was paused during a time when it was difficult to find schools willing to host it — but times have changed, and after numerous requests from the community, we are honored to once again host this incredible event.

The Thin Blue Line Football Game is much more than a rivalry on the field. It's an opportunity for our community and the law enforcement family to stand together in remembrance of the brave men and women who made the ultimate sacrifice in the line of duty.

Most importantly, it's a chance to honor and support the surviving families of our fallen officers and first responders — reminding them that their loved ones are never forgotten.
